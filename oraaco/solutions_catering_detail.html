Order Entry Form

#01
The Problem

Caterers write orders on paper or WhatsApp messages — leading to lost orders, wrong quantities, and missed details when things get busy.

Our Solution

A structured digital order form captures customer name, event date, menu items, quantities, delivery address, and special instructions — all saved instantly and never lost.

LIVE NOW

Bill Generation

#02
The Problem

Creating bills manually on paper or in Excel takes time, looks unprofessional, and makes it nearly impossible to track what's been paid or pending.

Our Solution

Auto-generate professional GST-ready invoices from any order in one tap. Share as PDF via WhatsApp or email instantly — no manual calculations needed.

LIVE NOW

Inventory Check

#03
The Problem

Caterers often realise mid-preparation that an ingredient is out of stock — causing delays, last-minute panic buying, and poor customer experience.

Our Solution

Real-time ingredient inventory tracker automatically deducts stock when orders are confirmed and alerts you when items fall below minimum thresholds — before it's too late.

LIVE NOW

Payment & Ledger

#04
The Problem

Tracking who paid, how much, and what method they used is a nightmare — especially across dozens of orders a week. Many caterers end up losing money they forgot to collect.

Our Solution

A complete payment ledger logs every transaction — cash, UPI, bank transfer — against each order. See exactly who owes you and generate receivable reports in seconds.

LIVE NOW

Customer & Order History

#05
The Problem

When a repeat customer calls, caterers can't quickly recall what they ordered last time, what they liked, or whether they had any payment issues — making every interaction feel cold.

Our Solution

A full customer profile stores order history, preferences, contact details, and billing records. Instantly know your customer the moment they call — deliver a personalised experience every time.

LIVE NOW

Basic Reports

#06
The Problem

Most caterers have no idea which menu items are most profitable, which months are slowest, or how much revenue they actually made — making it impossible to grow strategically.

Our Solution

Auto-generated daily, weekly, and monthly reports show revenue, top-selling dishes, order volumes, and pending payments — giving you the numbers to make smarter decisions.

LIVE NOW

Calendar

#07
The Problem

Double-booking events or forgetting an upcoming large order happens more than caterers admit. Without a visual calendar, it's easy to over-commit and under-deliver.

Our Solution

An integrated event calendar maps every confirmed order to its delivery date. See your week at a glance, spot conflicts instantly, and plan staff and ingredients well in advance.

LIVE NOW

Daily Reminder & Order Check

#08
The Problem

In the rush of daily operations, follow-up calls, pending confirmations, and tomorrow's prep tasks get forgotten — leading to last-minute scrambles and unhappy customers.

Our Solution

Smart daily push notifications remind you of orders due tomorrow, pending payments, unconfirmed bookings, and low-stock items — so your mornings start organised, not chaotic.

LIVE NOW

Payment Status & Purchase List

#09
The Problem

Going to the market without a clear list means forgetting items, over-buying, and spending more than needed. And without payment status visibility, chasing dues is manual and awkward.

Our Solution

Auto-generate a consolidated purchase/shopping list from upcoming orders — exactly what you need, in what quantity. Paired with a live payment status board showing paid, partial, and overdue orders at a glance.
